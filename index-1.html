About ALIGNPRO

Modern orthodontic care, built around clear aligners

ALIGNPRO CLEAR ALIGNERS is offered at Aswini Dental Clinic in Koyilandy, Kozhikode, with a focus on modern, aligner-based approaches to teeth alignment and smile correction.

Rather than a generic, one-size-fits-all approach, treatment planning is discussed with the dentist so patients understand what clear aligner treatment can involve for their individual case, from initial consultation through to ongoing review.

Whether you're exploring clear aligners for the first time or comparing them to traditional options, ALIGNPRO is a place to start the conversation with a qualified dental professional.

Services

Everything under ALIGNPRO Clear Aligners

A focused set of orthodontic services built around clear aligner treatment, from first consultation to long-term smile maintenance.

Clear Aligners

Custom-made, transparent trays designed to gradually guide teeth into a straighter position — worn discreetly through your daily routine.

Teeth Alignment

Assessment and planning aimed at guiding teeth toward a more even, well-aligned bite over the course of treatment.

Smile Correction

Addressing concerns like spacing and crowding, with a treatment path aimed at a more confident, balanced smile.

Orthodontic Consultation

An in-depth evaluation with the dentist to understand your smile goals and discuss an appropriate treatment path.

Retainers

Custom retainers to help support and maintain your smile once your aligner treatment is complete.

Ongoing Review

Progress check-ins through your treatment journey, so your plan can be reviewed and discussed as you go.
